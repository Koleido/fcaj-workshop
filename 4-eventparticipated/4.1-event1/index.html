Event 1

Report on the “Company Meeting on 06/06/2026”

Event Objectives

The meetup held on June 6th, 2026 aimed to introduce participants to a variety of practical technology topics and industry experiences. The event covered areas such as cloud computing, containerization, cybersecurity, artificial intelligence, real-time applications, teamwork, and career development. Through this event, participants were able to gain exposure to current technology trends and learn from professionals working in different domains of the IT industry.

Event Information

  • Time: 09:00 AM – 12:00 PM, June 6th, 2026
  • Attendance Method: In-person at the company office
  • Reference Materials: Presentation slides provided by the organizers in the folder “Meetup 06/06/2026”

Speakers and Presentation Topics

  • Bao Huynh – Docker – A Containerization Technology, Junior Cloud Native Developer at Endava Vietnam, Founder / Head Lab of ITea Lab

  • Le Hoang Gia Dai – WAF + ML for Cyber Attack Detection – Machine Learning-based Network Intrusion Detection System (NIDS) on AWS, ID: 2280618445, Team: AWS G3

  • Nguyen Quoc Bao – Multiplayer in the Cloud: Connecting Godot Clients with AWS WebSockets

  • Truong Huy Phuoc – The Art of Effective Teamwork

  • Viet Phat – GraphRAG: Building GraphRAG Applications Using Amazon Bedrock and Amazon Neptune

  • Tran Trung Vinh – From IT Helpdesk to Senior Sysadmin: A Practical Career Journey and the First Steps Toward Cloud and DevOps, System Administrator at Central Retail Group

Key Highlights

1. Docker – A Containerization Technology

This presentation introduced the concept of containerization and the role of Docker in modern software development. I learned how Docker packages applications and their dependencies into containers, allowing them to run consistently across different environments. The session also highlighted Docker’s importance in cloud-native architectures, DevOps workflows, and application deployment processes.

2. WAF + Machine Learning for Cyber Attack Detection

This topic focused on improving cybersecurity by combining Web Application Firewalls (WAF) with Machine Learning techniques. The speaker demonstrated how machine learning can be used to identify abnormal traffic patterns and support intrusion detection systems deployed on AWS. The session provided insight into how AI can enhance traditional security solutions and improve threat detection capabilities.

3. Multiplayer in the Cloud with AWS WebSockets

This presentation explored the development of real-time multiplayer applications using AWS WebSocket services and the Godot game engine. Through this session, I gained a basic understanding of how cloud infrastructure can support real-time communication between multiple clients and how WebSockets enable bidirectional communication in multiplayer environments.

4. The Art of Effective Teamwork

Unlike the highly technical sessions, this presentation focused on collaboration and communication within teams. The speaker shared practical experiences on task distribution, conflict resolution, communication strategies, and teamwork principles. This session emphasized that technical skills alone are not enough; effective collaboration is equally important for project success.

5. GraphRAG with Amazon Bedrock and Amazon Neptune

The GraphRAG presentation introduced a modern approach to building AI applications by combining Large Language Models with graph-based knowledge systems. The speaker demonstrated how Amazon Bedrock and Amazon Neptune can be integrated to create intelligent applications capable of retrieving and reasoning over structured knowledge. This session provided a valuable introduction to emerging AI technologies and knowledge graph applications.

6. From IT Helpdesk to Senior Sysadmin

This career-oriented session shared a real-world journey from an entry-level IT Helpdesk position to becoming a Senior System Administrator. The speaker discussed technical growth, continuous learning, professional development, and the transition into Cloud and DevOps. The presentation provided practical advice and motivation for students and newcomers pursuing careers in the IT industry.

Achievements

After attending the event, I achieved the following outcomes:

  • Participated in my first in-person technology meetup.
  • Gained exposure to multiple technology domains within a single event.
  • Improved my understanding of Docker, cloud computing, cybersecurity, AI applications, and teamwork.
  • Learned about real-world career development paths in the IT industry.
  • Collected presentation materials for future review and self-study.
  • Became more motivated to continue learning cloud technologies and related topics.

Challenges Encountered

As this was my first technical meetup, I encountered several challenges:

  • Some presentations covered advanced topics that were unfamiliar to me.
  • The amount of technical information shared within a short period was difficult to absorb completely.
  • Certain concepts, such as GraphRAG, machine learning-based intrusion detection, and cloud-based real-time communication, were new to me.
  • I did not yet have enough experience taking effective notes during technical presentations.

Despite these challenges, the provided slides allow me to review the material afterward and strengthen my understanding over time.

Lessons Learned

Through this meetup, I realized that professional growth requires continuous learning beyond classroom knowledge. Industry events provide opportunities to discover emerging technologies, understand real-world applications, and learn from experienced professionals.

I also learned that keeping up with technical discussions requires a solid foundation and consistent self-study. Even when I could not fully understand every topic during the event, exposure to these subjects helped broaden my perspective and identify areas that I would like to explore further.

Event Experience

The meetup on June 6th, 2026 was my first experience attending a technology-focused event in person. Although some technical topics were beyond my current level of knowledge, I was impressed by the professional atmosphere and the willingness of speakers to share their expertise and experiences.

Listening to presentations on cloud computing, AI, cybersecurity, containerization, and career development helped me gain a broader understanding of the technology industry. The event also motivated me to improve my technical foundation so that I can better engage with future technical discussions and workshops.

In addition, I recognized the importance of reviewing materials after attending events. The presentation slides provided by the organizers will serve as valuable resources for further learning and deeper exploration of the topics discussed.
